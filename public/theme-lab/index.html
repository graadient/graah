The shield,
modernised.

The shield stays the primary mark, and the horns go back to being an optional crest. Below are fourteen modern, simplified versions of the arms. Each one keeps the two things that make it ours: the rainbow arc and the three stars. Everything else could change: the shield's outline, the line weight, the colour, the material and the angle of the arc.

→

Always kept

Three bands in blue, gold and red, in that order from the top, arching upwards. Three six-pointed stars, two above the arc and one below. Red stars on a silver field whenever there's colour.

Free to change

The shield's outline (heater, kite, U, chamfered, round). Outlines or none. How deep the arc is and how thick the bands are. How sharp the stars are. The material: flat, enamel, light, engraved.

How to read the page

01 is the gallery: each version large, then at 48, 32 and 16px. 02 is the stress test: colour, paper and one colour. 03 lets you tap through every version in the hero, the seal, a letterhead and a browser tab.
