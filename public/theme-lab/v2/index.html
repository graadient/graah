You picked C · Forge as the base and wanted parts of A · Bifröst. B · Gustavian and D · Meridian are dropped. Below are five ways to combine the two. Each moves the same dials: ground, metal, display face, how the arc shows up, how the runes are treated, and how much ornament. They run from pure forge to cold and futurist, plus a daylight version for reading.

What changed since v0.1

The crest horns are redrawn with their true tinctures. The cord has real over-and-under crossings. Fjord-steel blue is back in every palette, so the arms' blue, gold and red always appear together.

Retired: the ᚼ star as a logo

The six-armed hagall rune I proposed as the everyday mark was also used by the SS (on the Totenkopf ring) and is still used by far-right groups today. That's a bad fit for a family whose chronicle includes Ravensbrück and the resistance. It stays only inside the name ᚴᚱᛅᛅᚼ, and the horns-and-star mark replaces it.

Marks to carry forward

Whatever palette wins, these pieces travel with it. All of them come from the real arms and crest, not invented ornament.

New everyday mark · Horns & star

The crest's two horns cradling one star from the shield. Unique to Graah, readable at 16 px, and Norse without borrowing anyone else's symbol. Replaces the ᚼ star from v1.

At favicon & app-icon size

Same mark on four grounds, 64 → 24 px. The two-tone horns stay legible; the star keeps it from reading as just “horns”.

The full arms · ceremony only

Filled shield for print, seal and the hero. Monoline shield for screens and the futurist directions. Use the shield sparingly so it keeps its weight.
